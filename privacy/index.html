Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Pawssport iOS app ("Pawssport", "we", "our") handles information. We built Pawssport with privacy as a default — your pet's data stays on your device.

Information We Collect

Pawssport does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on remote servers. All data you enter — including pet profiles, vaccination records, medications, appointments, photos, and reminders — is stored locally on your device.

We do not require an account, email address, or login to use the app.

How Your Data Is Used

The information you enter is used solely to provide the app's features on your device, such as displaying your pet's records and scheduling local notifications.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Pawssport schedules reminders locally on your device using iOS's notification system. These reminders never leave your device.

Photos

If you add a photo of your pet, Pawssport requests access to your photo library only to import the photo you select. Photos you import are stored locally within the app.

Third Parties

Pawssport does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

iCloud Backup

Like most iOS apps, Pawssport's local data may be included in your device's iCloud backup if you have iCloud Backup enabled. That backup is managed by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy. We do not have access to it.

Children's Privacy

Pawssport is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

Because all data is on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing entries inside the app or by deleting the app, which removes all associated data.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@mypawssport.com.
